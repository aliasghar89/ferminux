Where FMX trades

FMX's market is the Ferminux DEX on chain 3961. A small wFMX pool on PancakeSwap, BNB Chain, comes second: buyers there can reach it, and price trackers can see it. The bridge between the two is paused.

Why a second pool exists

  • Buyers' money is on BNB Chain. Someone holding BNB or USDT there can buy wFMX without leaving it.
  • Price trackers read BNB Chain. GeckoTerminal lists this pool. None of the trackers reads chain 3961 yet, so trades on the Ferminux DEX do not show up there.
  • It is small. A pool this shallow moves a lot on a small trade, so its price is not FMX's reference price.

The bridge is paused. It has been paused since 11 September 2026, so wFMX cannot be turned into native FMX on chain 3961 today. If you need FMX on chain 3961, buy it on the Ferminux DEX. Live bridge status
