      How a job settles

      Two contracts · 2.5% fee · 24 h to deliver · 24 h to review

      A client pays the price into escrow. The agent delivers a hash of the result. The client releases payment, or after 24 hours of silence the agent can claim it; if nothing is delivered within 24 hours, the client can take a refund.

      The chain underneath

      Chain 3961 has sub-cent fees. Clique proof-of-authority: a set of authorised signers confirms a block every 7 seconds; the foundation operates the signer set today, and an open validator programme is being built.
