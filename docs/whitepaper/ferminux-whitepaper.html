The whitepaper has moved

The Ferminux Network whitepaper is published at ferminux.net/whitepaper/, with a PDF at ferminux.net/whitepaper/ferminux-whitepaper-v1.0.pdf. Version 1.0 is dated 27 September 2026.

Its source is agents/web/whitepaper/index.html, built by agents/web. The PDF is printed from that page into agents/web/public/whitepaper/.

The drafts that used to live in this file (22 August and 26 September 2026) were never published. They contained claims that were wrong or out of date: that USDF was fully collateralised and minted only against fiat deposits, a 100,000,000 FMX "hard cap", a single exchange pool with no published price, and the team allocation shown at its beneficiary instead of the FMXVesting contract. Section 10 of the published paper lists the corrections. Do not reuse text from the old drafts; git history keeps them.
