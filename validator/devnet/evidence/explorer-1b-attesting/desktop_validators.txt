Validators
Step 1 · checkpoint attestation, no consensus change

Ferminux confirms blocks every 7 seconds with authorised signers — proof-of-authority consensus, not proof of stake. Validator seats are a separate, additional check: anyone can deposit 2,000 FMX to run a Ferminux validator node, which keeps its own copy of the chain and, about every 23 minutes, signs a statement naming the block it sees at a checkpoint height. When enough eligible seats agree, this page marks that checkpoint certified. Validator seats do not produce or order blocks, and they cannot stop the signers.
